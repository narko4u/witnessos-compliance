| Response | Action |
|----------|--------|

## 4. Qualification Criteria & Scoring Matrix

### Qualification Criteria

Prospective design partners are assessed against seven weighted criteria. Minimum score for alpha admission: **70/100**.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What We're Looking For | Scoring |
|---|-----------|--------|------------------------|---------|
| 1 | **Regulatory Urgency** | 20% | Active regulatory pressure (APRA, ASIC, EU AI Act, SEC) creating immediate need for agent compliance | 1 (none) → 5 (active enforcement/audit) |
| 2 | **Agent Deployment Maturity** | 20% | Currently operating autonomous agents in production, or actively building agent workflows | 1 (no plans) → 5 (production agents, >100 actions/day) |
| 3 | **Strategic Reference Value** | 15% | Brand credibility, market influence, and industry vertical weight | 1 (unrecognised) → 5 (top-5 in sector, globally known) |
| 4 | **Engagement Feasibility** | 15% | Likelihood of signing within 4 weeks, decision-maker access, internal champion identified | 1 (gatekept, no champion) → 5 (CEO/VP sponsor, fast legal) |
| 5 | **Technical Fit** | 15% | Agent architecture compatibility (API-first, Python/Go stack, cloud-native) | 1 (mainframe/COBOL) → 5 (modern microservices, API-native) |
| 6 | **Feedback Quality Potential** | 10% | Ability to provide structured, detailed product feedback (dedicated eng team, testing discipline) | 1 (no eng bandwidth) → 5 (dedicated test/integration team) |
| 7 | **Network & Partnership Potential** | 5% | Ability to introduce us to other high-value targets, channel distribution, or co-product opportunity | 1 (isolated) → 5 (ecosystem leader, can open doors) |

### Stage Transition Checklist

**Stage 1 → 2 (Contacted):**
- [ ] Personalised email drafted using appropriate template
- [ ] LinkedIn profile reviewed for mutual connections
- [ ] Warm intro path explored (investors, advisors, alumni network)
- [ ] Target's role confirmed as correct decision-maker
- [ ] Email sent, tracked (read receipts / CRM)

**Stage 2 → 3 (Responded):**
- [ ] Response logged in Notes column
- [ ] Follow-up sequence activated based on response type
- [ ] If positive: schedule discovery call within 7 days

**Stage 3 → 4 (Qualifying):**
- [ ] Discovery call completed (see qualification criteria above)
- [ ] Scoring matrix filled with call insights
- [ ] Internal go/no-go decision documented
- [ ] If score ≥ 70: proceed to NDA stage
- [ ] If score < 70: tag as "Deferred" with rationale

**Stage 4 → 5 (NDA Stage):**
- [ ] Mutual NDA sent (using template at `../MUTUAL_NDA.md`)
- [ ] Counterparty signing authority confirmed
- [ ] Executed NDA filed

**Stage 5 → 6 (Agreement Sent):**
- [ ] Design Partner Agreement drafted using `partner-kit/DESIGN_PARTNER_AGREEMENT.md`
- [ ] Partner-specific terms reviewed (duration, contact, data handling)
- [ ] Agreement sent to counterparty

**Stage 6 → 7 (Signed):**
- [ ] Agreement executed by both parties
- [ ] Welcome email sent with credentials and onboarding pack
- [ ] Partner added to Slack channel
- [ ] First weekly sync scheduled

**Stage 7 → 8 (Onboarding):**
- [ ] ONBOARDING_CHECKLIST.md initiated
- [ ] Gateway credentials provisioned
- [ ] Technical integration support assigned
