# Partner Engagement Model — WitnessOS Alpha

## 1. What Partners Get

- **Free gateway access** — no-cost access to WitnessOS gateway infrastructure during the alpha program.
- **Dedicated Slack channel** — private channel with direct access to our engineering and product team.
- **Weekly sync** — 30-minute standing call each week to review progress, surface blockers, and prioritise upcoming work.
- **Roadmap priority** — your use cases and feature requests are prioritised on the roadmap ahead of the public queue.
- **Early access** — preview builds and new capabilities before general release.
- **Co-branded case study** — jointly authored case study for publication at programme close.
- **Public recognition** — listing and/or joint announcement on our website and social channels (with your explicit permission).

## 2. What We Need

- **Operational feedback** — honest assessment of gateway reliability, latency, and deployment friction in your environment.
- **Bug reports** — timely reporting of any issues encountered, with reproduction steps where possible.
- **Use case validation** — real-world testing against your production or staging workflows so we validate assumptions.
- **Time commitment** — roughly 4+ hours per week from a technical point of contact.
- **Prompt eval reviews** — qualitative assessment of witness prompts and evaluation accuracy.
- **Reference calls** — availability for 1–2 calls with prospective partners (post-alpha) if your experience is positive.

## 3. Timeline

| Phase | Activities |
|---|---|
| **Week 1 — Legal & Setup** | Sign NDA/agreement; provision environment; distribute credentials; onboarding call. |
| **Week 2 — Integration & Test** | Deploy gateway; connect data sources; run initial test cases; validate connectivity. |
| **Week 3 — Operational Review** | Real-world operation; collect telemetry; structured feedback session; mid-point retro. |
| **Week 4 — Closeout** | Final review; handover of outcomes; co-branded materials draft; program wrap. |

## 4. Evidence Grades (Alpha)

Feedback and findings are classified by evidence grade to calibrate confidence:

| Grade | Definition |
|---|---|
| **E0** | Anecdotal observation — no structured data or reproduction. |
| **E1** | Single controlled test or documented walkthrough. |
| **E2** | Repeated observation across multiple environments or sustained operation. |
| **E3** | Verified by automated tests and independently reproducible by engineering. |

## 5. Exit

Either party may terminate with **30 days' written notice**. At closeout the partner receives a complete summary of findings and co-branded assets. Empirical evidence (logs, evals, metrics) is retained by Empire Labs for product improvement; any proprietary partner data is deleted within 90 days of termination.
