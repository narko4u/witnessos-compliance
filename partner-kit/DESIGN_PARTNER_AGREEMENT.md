### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a collaborative design partnership for the development, testing, and refinement of autonomous agent governance technology (the "**WitnessOS Platform**"). This Agreement governs the terms under which Partner may participate in an invitation-only alpha programme and establishes mutual confidentiality obligations for information exchanged during that participation.

This Agreement incorporates the confidentiality framework set out in the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the Parties (reference: **MUTUAL_NDA.md** at `../MUTUAL_NDA.md`), which shall apply to all Confidential Information exchanged under this Agreement as if fully set out herein.

### 2. Scope of Participation

**(a) Invitation-Only Alpha.** Participation in the Design Partner programme is by invitation only. Partner acknowledges that this is a limited, pre-release alpha programme and not a public offering. Company reserves the right to accept or decline any prospective Partner in its sole discretion.

**(b) Programme Duration.** The initial alpha programme term is **two (2) to four (4) weeks** commencing on the date first written above, unless extended by mutual written agreement of the Parties.

**(c) Maximum Participants.** The alpha programme is limited to a maximum of three (3) enterprise-grade participants (the "**E3 Cohort**") at any one time. Partner acknowledges that Company may admit additional Partners at its discretion, up to this cap.

**(d) Platform Access.** Company will provide Partner with access to a pre-release instance of the WitnessOS Platform solely for evaluation, testing, and feedback purposes. This access is non-exclusive, non-transferable, and limited to employees and contractors of Partner who have a genuine need to evaluate the Platform for the Purpose.

### 3. Confidentiality

The Parties agree to be bound by the mutual confidentiality obligations set out in the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement referenced in Section 1. For the avoidance of doubt:

(a) **Company Confidential Information** includes but is not limited to: software architecture, source code, algorithms, security models, test results, system designs, business plans, pricing, and any feedback or derivative analysis generated by Company arising from the Design Partner programme.

(b) **Partner Confidential Information** includes but is not limited to: internal systems architecture, workflow data (in anonymised or raw form where shared), security policies, evaluation results, and business requirements disclosed to Company.

(c) Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care.

(d) Notwithstanding any other provision, nothing in this Agreement restricts Company from using generalised insights, aggregated and de-identified data, or lessons learned from the Design Partner programme to improve the WitnessOS Platform, provided that no Partner Confidential Information is disclosed.

### 4. Licence

**(a) Free Licence During Alpha.** Company grants Partner a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free, revocable licence** to access and use the WitnessOS Platform solely for the Purpose during the alpha programme term. This licence is expressly limited to the alpha period and does not extend to any post-alpha or commercial version of the Platform.

**(b) No Implied Rights.** Except as expressly set out in this Section 4, no intellectual property rights are granted or implied by this Agreement. All right, title, and interest in and to the WitnessOS Platform, including all modifications, improvements, and derivative works thereof, remain solely with Company.

**(c) Reservation of Rights.** Company reserves all rights not expressly granted herein. Partner shall not: (i) sublicense, sell, rent, lease, transfer, or distribute access to the Platform; (ii) reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Platform; (iii) use the Platform to develop a competing product or service; or (iv) exceed the authorised user scope set out in Section 2(d).

### 5. Feedback

**(a) Grant of Feedback.** Partner may, and is encouraged to, provide suggestions, enhancement requests, recommendations, bug reports, or other feedback regarding the WitnessOS Platform (collectively, "**Feedback**"). Partner hereby grants Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up licence to use, incorporate, and commercialise any Feedback for any purpose without restriction or obligation to Partner.

**(b) No Obligation.** Company is under no obligation to incorporate or act upon any Feedback provided by Partner.

**(c) Non-Confidential.** Unless expressly marked as Confidential Information at the time of disclosure, Feedback shall not be treated as Confidential Information under this Agreement.

### 6. Data Ownership

**(a) Partner Retains Ownership.** As between the Parties, Partner retains all right, title, and interest in and to all data, information, and materials that Partner submits, uploads, or otherwise provides to the WitnessOS Platform during the alpha programme ("**Partner Data**"). Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Partner Data to Company.

**(b) Licence to Operate.** Partner grants Company a non-exclusive, limited, revocable licence to access, use, process, and store Partner Data solely to provide the WitnessOS Platform services under this Agreement. This licence terminates upon the expiration or termination of this Agreement, subject to Section 6(c).

**(c) Data Deletion.** Within **thirty (30) days** following termination of this Agreement, Company shall, at Partner's written direction and to the extent technically practicable, either: (i) return Partner Data in a commonly used machine-readable format; or (ii) securely delete all copies of Partner Data from the Platform and certify in writing that deletion is complete. Company may retain one archival copy for legal compliance purposes only, which shall remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

**(d) Aggregated Data.** Company may use aggregated, de-identified data derived from Partner Data for analytics, benchmarking, and product improvement purposes, provided that such data cannot reasonably be used to identify Partner or any natural person.

### 7. Term and Termination

**(a) Term.** This Agreement commences on the date first written above and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 7.

**(b) Termination Without Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other Party.

**(c) Termination for Convenience by Company.** Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if, in its sole discretion, the Design Partner programme is restructured, discontinued, or migrated to a different phase.

**(d) Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (i) all licences granted under this Agreement immediately cease; (ii) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information in accordance with Section 7 of the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement; (iii) Partner Data shall be handled in accordance with Section 6(c); and (iv) Sections 3 (Confidentiality), 4(b)–(c), 5(a), 6, 8, 9, and 10 shall survive termination.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Queensland, Australia**. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Queensland and the Federal Court of Australia.

### 10. General Provisions

**(a) Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement referenced in Section 1, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior communications, representations, or agreements, whether written or oral.

**(b) Amendments.** Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

**(c) No Agency.** Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between the Parties.

**(d) Waiver.** No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver thereof.
