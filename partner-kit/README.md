# WitnessOS Design Partner Kit

**Phase F — Invitation-Only Alpha Program**

---

## Overview

The **WitnessOS Design Partner Kit** is your exclusive gateway to the first-ever deployment of the WitnessOS evidence lifecycle platform. This is not a public beta, a community trial, or a sandbox. It is a tightly scoped, high-signal collaboration between Empire Labs and a select group of design partners who operate at the highest evidentiary standards.

WitnessOS is a private, purpose-built infrastructure platform for capturing, preserving, and verifying digital evidence at **E3 max** forensic grade. It replaces fragmented toolchains, chain-of-custody spreadsheets, and manual audit logs with a single cryptographic pipeline from ingestion through preservation to independent verification.

---

## Value Proposition

Design partners in this alpha will:

- **Shape the product.** Your workflows, edge cases, and domain expertise directly determine WitnessOS capability priorities. Every core feature is being validated against real operational requirements.
- **Set the standard.** As an inaugural design partner, your evidence workflows become reference implementations that inform certification, compliance, and onboarding for all future deployments.
- **Operate at E3 max.** The platform enforces strict chain-of-custody, tamper-evident logging, and cryptographically sealed preservation — meeting evidentiary standards that satisfy regulatory, civil, and criminal admissibility requirements.
- **Secure priority access.** Phase F partners receive direct engineering support, expedited feature requests, and a dedicated escalation path. No ticket queues. No generic SLAs.
